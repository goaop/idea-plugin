Reports advice methods that the Go! AOP 4.0 aspect loader rejects with an AspectException:
  • a method with #[Before], #[After], #[Around] or #[AfterThrowing] that is not public: generated proxies call advices as first-class callables on the aspect instance;
  • a method with more than one advice attribute: split it into one method per advice. #[Pointcut] may still be combined with an advice.

The quick-fix makes the advice method public.
